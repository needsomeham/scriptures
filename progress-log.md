# Progress log (for Jacob)

Lightweight tracker, not a journal — keep a personal journal for
reflections/notes if you like, and just tick the stage boxes here so the
vault (and any agent working in it) knows where you actually are.

## Stage checklists
(copied verbatim from each stage's "Ready for next stage when" line in
[[learning-path]] — check off when true, don't unlock ahead of it)

### Ready for Stage 1
- [ ] You can name/write all 24 letters and diacritics from memory.
- [ ] STEP Bible and Logeion are bookmarked.

### Ready for Stage 2
- [ ] You can parse any regular verb/noun form.
- [ ] You recognize the moods/voices.
- [ ] You can read a simplified/glossed NT sentence without stopping
      every word.

### Ready for Stage 3
- [ ] You can read a chapter of 1 John or Mark with the reader's edition
      at conversational pace (occasional lookups, not constant).
- [ ] You understand what a footnote/apparatus marker is pointing at,
      even if you can't yet resolve it yourself.
