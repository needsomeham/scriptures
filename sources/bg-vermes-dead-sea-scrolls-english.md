**In one line:** The standard accessible English translation of the Dead Sea Scrolls — the primary-source anchor for Second Temple Judaism.
**What it is:** Geza Vermes (1924–2013), Oxford Professor of Jewish Studies and one of the original scroll scholars, produced this Penguin Classics translation, now in its 7th edition (2011), covering nearly the full non-biblical scroll corpus in readable English with brief introductions per text.
**Use in the path:** Sample directly — read the Community Rule (1QS) and a Pesher (e.g. on Habakkuk) alongside [[second-temple-judaism]] and [[jewish-apocalyptic]] to see sectarian Jewish self-understanding contemporary with the NT.
**Strengths:** Highly readable, scholarly credibility, organized by genre with helpful short intros.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** Vermes's introductions reflect his own (sometimes minimized-messianism) reading of Qumran texts; for a text-critical alternative translation see Florentino García Martínez's *The Dead Sea Scrolls Translated* (Brill/Eerdmans), which is more literal and closer to critical editions — cross-check a striking translation choice against it before treating Vermes's wording as decisive.
**Claims to note:** Qumran community = Essenes — **majority view**, with **minority view** dissent (some scholars propose a broader "Sadducean" or mixed-origin community).
