**In one line:** A fresh critical Greek NT edition from Tyndale House with running footnote glosses of rare vocabulary, designed to be read rather than parsed.
**What it is:** Edited by Dirk Jongkind and Peter Williams (Tyndale House, Cambridge) with an editorial method distinct from NA28/UBS5 (weighting documented manuscript readings and paragraphing differently); the reader's edition adds page-bottom glosses for words occurring under ~25 times, letting a beginner read continuously.
**Use in the path:** Stage 1–2 spine text. Start 1 John and Mark in this edition once the alphabet is secure — the footnote glosses remove the need to stop and look up every rare word, which is the whole point of reading-first acquisition.
**Strengths:** Purpose-built for reading, respected critical apparatus, compact single volume.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** Its textual choices differ in places from NA28/UBS5 (documented, not idiosyncratic) — a textual-criticism note should compare; not paired with English on the page, so total beginners may still want STEP Bible open alongside for the first weeks.
**Claims to note:** Text-critical methodology (favoring earliest documented reading over reasoned eclecticism in some places) is a **minority view** relative to NA28/UBS5's mainstream reasoned-eclectic method; both are T1 critical editions, difference is method not error.
