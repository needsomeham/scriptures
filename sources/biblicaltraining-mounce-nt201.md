**In one line:** A full free video course by Bill Mounce, hosted on the nonprofit BiblicalTraining.org platform, following the same first-year syllabus as his textbook.
**What it is:** BiblicalTraining.org (founded by Bill Mounce; faculty from institutions like Denver Seminary, Gordon-Conwell contribute other courses) hosts complete recorded lecture courses for free. NT201 is Mounce's own first-year Greek sequence, effectively the classroom version of [[billmounce-site]]'s videos.
**Use in the path:** Stage 2 (Core Grammar) — the recommended video-course track for a learner who wants a full "sit in the lecture hall" experience rather than piecemeal YouTube clips. Follow week-by-week alongside reading in 1 John/Mark.
**Strengths:** Free, structured like an actual course (syllabus, sequence), credentialed instructor.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** Requires free registration; production values are lecture-capture, not polished; same paradigm-first pedagogy caveats as the underlying textbook.
**Claims to note:** none load-bearing.
