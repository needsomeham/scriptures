**In one line:** A fresh, independently-edited 2017 critical text that leans closer to early manuscript spelling/paragraphing conventions than NA/UBS, free to read online.
**What it is:** Produced by Dirk Jongkind and Peter J. Williams at Tyndale House, Cambridge (2017); a genuinely new editorial pass (not a compilation of prior editions), following documented scribal-habit and orthographic principles, published by Crossway and free to read at thegreeknewtestament.com and on STEP Bible [T-04].
**Use in the path:** Read alongside SBLGNT/NA28 once past beginner stage to see where independent editorial judgment diverges from NA28/UBS5 — useful for noticing that "the" critical text is not monolithic.
**Strengths:** Genuinely independent editorial work (not derivative), transparent introduction explaining method, free access, retains ancient paragraphing/nomina sacra conventions that make manuscript habits visible.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** Smaller team and narrower apparatus than NA28/ECM; differs from NA28 in a few hundred places, mostly minor (word order, spelling) — don't mistake divergence for a rival "school."
**Claims to note:** A `majority view` reasoned-eclectic project, methodologically closer to "single-best-manuscript-first" scribal-habit reasoning than NA28's broader stemmatic weighting — a genuine, named methodological variation, not a fringe position.
