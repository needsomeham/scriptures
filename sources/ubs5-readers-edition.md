**In one line:** The standard UBS5 critical text with footnote glosses for infrequent words, the direct sibling of the widely-known Zondervan Reader's Greek New Testament.
**What it is:** Produced by the United Bible Societies / Deutsche Bibelgesellschaft (successor to Barclay Newman's original UBS3 reader's edition), pairing the same NA28-aligned critical text used in most modern seminary study with page-bottom glosses (words under ~30 occurrences) and abbreviated textual notes.
**Use in the path:** Stage 1–2, interchangeable with [[tyndale-house-greek-nt-readers-edition]] as the reading spine; pick one text tradition (UBS5/NA28 mainstream vs. Tyndale House's distinct method) and stay with it for consistency, cross-checking the other later once textual criticism becomes a topic.
**Strengths:** Matches the apparatus most commentaries and other reference tools cite (NA28/UBS5 numbering and text is the field standard); includes brief textual-note abbreviations, a soft on-ramp to textual criticism.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** Glossary entries give only a contextual gloss, not full lexical range — still needs a real lexicon ([[abbott-smith-lexicon]] or BDAG) once curiosity exceeds the footnote.
**Claims to note:** Reasoned-eclectic critical method is the **consensus** approach among mainstream text critics (vs. Tyndale House's documentary-priority method, a **minority view**); see [[moc-textual-criticism]].
