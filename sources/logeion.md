**In one line:** A free University of Chicago lexicon aggregator that shows LSJ, a Middle Liddell abridgment, and other dictionaries side by side for any Greek word.
**What it is:** Built and hosted by the University of Chicago's Department of Classics, Logeion merges the full Liddell-Scott-Jones (LSJ) lexicon with several smaller dictionaries and frequency data, searchable by lemma or inflected form.
**Use in the path:** Stage 3–4 (Reading Fluency onward) — once NT-specific lexica (Abbott-Smith, BDAG) leave a word's broader classical/Koine range unclear, Logeion shows how a word is used across all of Greek literature, which is exactly the "understand in context" habit this learner wants instead of flashcards.
**Strengths:** Free, fast, handles inflected forms directly, shows multiple dictionaries at once, includes frequency counts.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** LSJ is classics-oriented and pre-dates much NT-specific lexical scholarship (BDAG/Louw-Nida account for Koine-specific and Christian usage better); good for range-of-meaning, not a substitute for an NT lexicon on theological vocabulary.
**Claims to note:** none load-bearing.
