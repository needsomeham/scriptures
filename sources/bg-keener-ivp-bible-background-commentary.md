**In one line:** The fastest-to-use verse-by-verse background lookup across the entire NT.
**What it is:** Craig S. Keener (Asbury Theological Seminary, prolific NT background scholar) compiled this 2014 (2nd ed.) IVP Academic reference, giving a short historical/cultural background note for nearly every verse of the NT, drawing on Jewish and Greco-Roman primary sources.
**Use in the path:** Keep open while reading the Greek text in [[learning-path]] — look up unfamiliar customs, place-names, or social practices as they come up verse by verse, rather than reading it as a book.
**Strengths:** Comprehensive coverage, concise, well-cited to primary sources, genuinely fast to use as a reading companion.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** Notes are necessarily brief — treat as a pointer to go deeper (e.g. into [[bg-ferguson-backgrounds-early-christianity]] or Josephus directly) rather than a citable final word on a contested point; Keener's own scholarly commitments (broadly evangelical) occasionally shade word choice on disputed questions.
**Claims to note:** N/A at the note level — treat each entry's claim individually and verify against a T1 source before treating it as settled.
