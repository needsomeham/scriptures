**In one line:** Short daily videos that read one NT verse at a time in Greek, aimed at building a sustainable reading habit.
**What it is:** Created by Rob Plummer (PhD, professor of NT, Southern Baptist Theological Seminary), each 2–4 minute episode walks through a single verse's grammar and vocabulary in canonical order. Companion "Daily Dose of Hebrew" and reading plans exist too.
**Use in the path:** Stage 1 (Foundations, weeks 1–8) onward — the core reading-first daily habit once the alphabet is down; run in parallel with a reader's edition of 1 John or Mark so the verse discussed is the verse just read. Sustains momentum better than isolated grammar drills.
**Strengths:** Genuinely reading-first (whole verses, not flashcards), named credentialed instructor, free, low time-cost so it's sustainable daily.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** Pace is slow if used as a sole method (one verse/day); best as a supplement to independent reading, not a replacement for it.
**Claims to note:** none load-bearing.
