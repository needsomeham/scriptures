**In one line:** A complete free undergraduate NT course on video from a named Yale scholar — the single best free entry point in this shard.
**What it is:** Dale B. Martin (Yale, PhD Yale, historian of early Christianity) taught RLST 152; Yale recorded and released the full 26-lecture course (video, audio, transcripts) as part of Open Yale Courses, no login or cost.
**Use in the path:** Watch alongside reading each Gospel/epistle in Greek per [[learning-path]] — Martin lectures roughly in canonical/chronological order and explicitly teaches historical-critical method (Synoptic Problem, authorship debates, social setting), so pace lectures to match reading.
**Strengths:** Completely free, full transcripts (searchable), taught by a research-active named scholar, unusually candid about where scholarly consensus ends and Martin's own reading begins.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** Recorded 2009 — some bibliography is now dated; Martin's own historical-critical stance (e.g. on Pauline authenticity) tracks the Ehrman-style majority view and should be triangulated with [[bg-wright-bird-new-testament-in-its-world]] for the more traditional side.
**Claims to note:** Martin openly flags his classroom claims by confidence level in-lecture — good modeling of the claim-ladder habit this vault also uses.
