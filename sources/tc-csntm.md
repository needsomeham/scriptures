**In one line:** A free, high-resolution photographic archive of Greek NT manuscripts, founded by textual scholar Daniel B. Wallace — the most learner-friendly manuscript browsing tool.
**What it is:** CSNTM (csntm.org) was founded by Daniel B. Wallace (PhD, professor at Dallas Theological Seminary, prolific TC scholar) to photograph and digitize NT manuscripts worldwide, partnering with libraries/monasteries; its images and manuscript catalog are free to browse [T-08].
**Use in the path:** Good early/beginner entry point — before you can read an apparatus, browsing CSNTM's manuscript photos and short manuscript profiles builds intuition for what "a manuscript" physically is (uncial vs. minuscule script, page condition, marginal notes).
**Strengths:** Free, visually engaging, credentialed leadership, actively growing (Wallace/CSNTM have discovered/catalogued previously unphotographed manuscripts).
**Weaknesses / cautions:** A photo archive, not a critical edition or apparatus — doesn't itself adjudicate variants; Wallace's own public commentary sometimes ventures into apologetics-adjacent framing, so treat his interpretive claims (vs. the photo archive itself) as one voice among several.
**Claims to note:** n/a (archive) — Wallace's broader textual-reliability arguments in public talks are his own `majority view` synthesis, not a formal CSNTM position.
