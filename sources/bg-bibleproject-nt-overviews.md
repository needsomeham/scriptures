**In one line:** Free animated book-overview videos — good for structural orientation only, not a scholarly source.
**What it is:** BibleProject (founded by Tim Mackie, a former Western Seminary OT professor, and John Collins, animator) produces short (5–12 min) animated videos summarizing each NT book's literary structure and theological themes, free on YouTube and bibleproject.com.
**Use in the path:** Watch a book's overview video *before* starting to read it in Greek, purely to get the macro-structure (where the argument turns, major sections) — never cite it for a historical-critical claim.
**Strengths:** Free, visually clear on literary structure (chiasms, inclusios), genuinely helpful for seeing a book's shape before close reading.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** T3 tier — explicitly confessional/theological framing (assumes canonical unity and often harmonizes across traditions), no named credentialed presenter delivers on-camera critical claims, does not flag contested authorship/dating questions. Never use as the sole source for anything load-bearing; use only to orient, then verify any historical claim in [[bg-ehrman-new-testament-historical-introduction]] or [[bg-brown-introduction-to-new-testament]].
**Claims to note:** N/A — orientation only, no independent claims to adjudicate.
