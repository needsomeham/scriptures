**In one line:** The standard critical edition/translation of Second Temple Jewish apocalyptic and testamentary literature — the direct literary neighborhood of NT apocalyptic (esp. Revelation, Synoptic apocalyptic discourse, Jude).
**What it is:** James H. Charlesworth (Princeton Theological Seminary) edited this two-volume 1983/1985 collection (Yale UP, orig. Doubleday), gathering scholarly translations with introductions of texts like 1 Enoch, Jubilees, the Testaments of the Twelve Patriarchs, and 4 Ezra.
**Use in the path:** Read the introduction and relevant excerpts of 1 Enoch before/alongside Jude (which quotes it directly) and the Synoptic apocalyptic discourses — status: queued for full ingestion (two-volume, PDF/print-only per this vault's text-native rule; catalog via secondary discussion in [[bg-ferguson-backgrounds-early-christianity]] and [[jewish-apocalyptic]] until specific excerpts are sourced online).
**Strengths:** Comprehensive, scholarly introductions per text with date/provenance debates laid out; the field's reference standard.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** Expensive; some included texts have uncertain or late/Christian-interpolated transmission histories (introductions flag this per text) — treat any given text's pre-Christian dating as **contested** unless the specific intro says otherwise.
**Claims to note:** Jude's direct quotation of 1 Enoch 1:9 (Jude 14–15) — **consensus** that this is a direct citation of the Enochic text, not merely shared tradition.
