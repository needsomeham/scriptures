**In one line:** Live, small-group online Koine Greek classes taught synchronously by instructors, structured like a real academic term.
**What it is:** Ancient Language Institute runs live (not pre-recorded) online classes in Koine and Attic Greek and Latin, with named instructors holding graduate degrees in classics/biblical studies, using immersive/communicative methods influenced by the same comprehensible-input movement as [[biblical-language-center-living-koine-greek]].
**Use in the path:** Optional Stage 1–2 track for a learner who wants the accountability and live feedback of a real class rather than self-paced video — the "video/course-driven" option with the most classroom-like structure and cost to match.
**Strengths:** Live instruction and correction (unlike any recorded course), cohort accountability, communicative method builds intuitive fluency.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** Meaningfully more expensive than every other resource in this shard; fixed schedule requires a live-class commitment, which cuts against a self-paced reading-first plan.
**Claims to note:** none load-bearing.
