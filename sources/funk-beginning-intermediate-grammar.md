**In one line:** Robert Funk's linguistically rigorous, freely available grammar bridging beginning and intermediate Koine Greek.
**What it is:** Robert W. Funk (PhD Vanderbilt; co-author of the standard Blass-Debrunner-Funk reference grammar, BDF) wrote this three-volume grammar in the 1970s for the Society of Biblical Literature; it is now out of print but fully scanned and freely readable on the Internet Archive.
**Use in the path:** Stage 2–4 — a free alternative or supplement to Mounce/Decker/Croy for a learner comfortable with a denser, more linguistically technical presentation (a good fit for an engineering/CS background); also a bridge toward BDF, the standard T1 reference grammar for advanced syntax questions.
**Strengths:** Free, linguistically serious, written by a scholar who also produced the field's standard advanced reference grammar (BDF), so terminology carries forward.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** Dense and dated in presentation (1970s typesetting, less pedagogical scaffolding than modern textbooks); best as a second grammar for reference, not a first-time beginner's sole text.
**Claims to note:** none load-bearing.
