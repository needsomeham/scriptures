**In one line:** Free, searchable public-domain translation of the essential first-century Jewish historian for NT political/religious background.
**What it is:** Flavius Josephus (c. 37–100 CE), a Jewish priest-turned-Roman-client historian, wrote *Antiquities of the Jews* and *The Jewish War*. William Whiston's 1737 English translation is public domain and hosted, fully searchable, at Perseus (Tufts) and mirrored at penelope.uchicago.edu and archive.org.
**Use in the path:** Grep specific passages when a NT background question is historical (Herod's building projects, the Sanhedrin's composition, the Jewish revolt, Pilate, John the Baptist, James "brother of the Lord") — read the relevant chapter directly rather than relying on secondary paraphrase, consistent with this vault's synopsize-not-republish rule for *your* notes.
**Strengths:** Free, complete, searchable; Perseus links Greek-adjacent reference tools; irreplaceable as the single largest narrative source for the period.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** Whiston's 1737 English is archaic and occasionally imprecise by modern text-critical standards; Josephus himself has a political agenda (pro-Roman, pro-priestly) — corroborate any load-bearing historical claim with a second source (e.g. [[bg-sanders-judaism-practice-belief]] or [[bg-ferguson-backgrounds-early-christianity]]) rather than treating Josephus as neutral reportage.
**Claims to note:** Testimonium Flavianum (Josephus's passage mentioning Jesus, *Antiquities* 18.3.3) — authenticity of parts of the passage is **contested**; **majority view** holds a partially authentic core with later Christian interpolation.
