**In one line:** A free, born-digital critical Greek NT text, ideal as the everyday reading text before you're ready for NA28's apparatus.
**What it is:** Produced by the Society of Biblical Literature with Logos Bible Software (2010); editor Michael W. Holmes documented his editorial choices against a defined set of prior editions rather than compiling a fresh apparatus. Freely downloadable in Unicode text, with a companion apparatus module (SBLGNT Apparatus) noting where it departs from a baseline set of editions [T-03].
**Use in the path:** Default reading text from early Greek (month 2-3 onward) through intermediate — no license fee, clean Unicode font, works with any reader/parser. Switch primary study text to NA28 once ready for full-apparatus work.
**Strengths:** Free, well-documented editorial rationale, widely supported by open tools (Perseus-adjacent readers, Logos, many Greek learning apps); good bridge before NA28.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** Not a fresh critical edition — it's a documented eclectic compilation from existing editions, so it doesn't carry independent manuscript-level authority the way NA/UBS or THGNT do; its "apparatus" only shows disagreement with a handful of prior editions, not full manuscript variants.
**Claims to note:** Presented by its editor as a `majority view` reasoned-eclectic text, transparent about method rather than claiming novel textual research.
