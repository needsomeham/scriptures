**In one line:** A long-running, scholar-run group blog tracking current TC news, manuscript discoveries, and debates in near-real time — orient here, then verify against T1 sources.
**What it is:** A group blog (evangelicaltextualcriticism.blogspot.com) with contributors including Peter J. Gurry, Elijah Hixson, Peter Head, Tommy Wasserman, and other working TC scholars, active since the mid-2000s; despite the name, contributors span confessional and non-confessional positions and the content is scholarly in practice, not devotional [T-16].
**Use in the path:** Use throughout TC study as a current-awareness feed (new papyri finds, conference notes, book reviews, informal debate over CBGM/Byzantine priority) — never as a terminal citation for a load-bearing claim; follow its links back to the T1 book/article it references.
**Strengths:** Fast-moving, written by actual practicing scholars (not anonymous), often the first place a new manuscript discovery or edition gets discussed publicly; useful running commentary on [[tc-byzantine-priority-debate]] and [[tc-cbgm]] debates.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** Per this vault's rules, blogs are always T3/orient-only regardless of author credentials — individual posts vary in rigor and are not peer-reviewed; the site's "Evangelical" framing does not mean uniform theological or methodological agreement among contributors.
**Claims to note:** n/a as a source in itself — treat any scholarly claim found here as needing a T1/T2 citation before it's load-bearing in a note.
