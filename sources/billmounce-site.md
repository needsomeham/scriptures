**In one line:** Bill Mounce's own free hub — video lectures, vocabulary tools, and a weekly "Monday with Mounce" grammar column.
**What it is:** Companion site to [[mounce-basics-of-biblical-greek]], run by Mounce himself (PhD Aberdeen). Hosts free full video-lecture playlists working through the grammar chapter by chapter, plus flashcard/vocab apps and short exegetical notes.
**Use in the path:** Stage 2 (Core Grammar) — watch lectures alongside the textbook for a course-driven learner; the video-driven complement to reading-first study. Also useful in Stage 3 for the "Monday with Mounce" close readings of individual NT words in context.
**Strengths:** Free, credentialed, matches the book chapter-for-chapter, low production friction (short videos).
**Weaknesses / cautions:** Vocabulary apps encourage list-memorization over contextual acquisition — use them only to reinforce words already met while reading.
**Claims to note:** none load-bearing.
