**In one line:** A compact, single-semester-paced alternative to Mounce, reaching a full overview of NT Greek morphology faster and with a lighter apparatus.
**What it is:** N. Clayton Croy (PhD Emory; professor of NT, Trinity Lutheran Seminary/Methodist Theological School in Ohio) wrote this primer for one-semester intensive courses; it moves faster than Mounce and includes reading exercises drawn from the GNT earlier.
**Use in the path:** Stage 2 alternative to Mounce/Decker for a learner who wants a leaner, faster-paced grammar rather than a two-book grammar+workbook system — worth comparing tables of contents before committing, since switching mid-stream costs time.
**Strengths:** Compact, efficient pacing, reaches real text sooner than Mounce.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** Faster pace means less repetition/reinforcement built in — a self-paced learner without a class deadline may prefer Mounce's or Decker's slower on-ramp.
**Claims to note:** none load-bearing.
