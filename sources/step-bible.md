**In one line:** A free scholarly Bible-study platform from Tyndale House, Cambridge, with tagged Greek texts, morphology, and lexicon lookups for reading-in-context.
**What it is:** Built and maintained by Tyndale House (an independent biblical-research institute, Cambridge), STEP (Scripture Tools for Every Person) layers interlinear glosses, parsing, and Strong's/lexicon links over NA28-aligned Greek text, plus original-language search across the whole NT.
**Use in the path:** Stage 1–3 — the default free "look up this word/parsing without leaving the reading" companion to a printed reader's edition ([[tyndale-house-greek-nt-readers-edition]] or [[ubs5-readers-edition]]). Good for verifying a guess about a form rather than as the primary reading surface.
**Strengths:** Free, academically maintained, morphology + multiple lexicons in one place, no ads.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** Interface has a learning curve; like any interlinear tool, over-reliance can substitute for actually parsing forms yourself — use as a check, not a crutch. A simpler free alternative for quick lookups is Bible Hub's interlinear (biblehub.com/interlinear), which is convenient but uncredentialed (T3, orient only — verify anything load-bearing against STEP or a lexicon).
**Claims to note:** none load-bearing.
