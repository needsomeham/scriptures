**In one line:** A subscription app teaching biblical Greek and Hebrew through comprehensible-input-style exercises and spaced repetition rather than flashcard lists alone.
**What it is:** A commercial ed-tech app (Biblingo LLC); marketing emphasizes "reading with ancient eyes" and contextual acquisition, but public materials do not foreground the academic credentials of its curriculum authors the way Mounce's or BLC's do — treat as **orient only** until a named scholar's methodology write-up is found.
**Use in the path:** Optional Stage 1–2 supplement for a learner who wants gamified daily practice on a phone between reading sessions; not a substitute for a reader's edition or grammar.
**Strengths:** Designed around contextual sentences rather than bare word lists, mobile-friendly, matches this learner's stated preference for context over rote lists.
**Weaknesses / cautions:** Subscription cost is ongoing; T3 pending verification of curriculum authorship/credentials — do not cite its grammatical claims as authoritative without cross-checking a T1/T2 grammar.
**Claims to note:** none verified as load-bearing scholarship.
