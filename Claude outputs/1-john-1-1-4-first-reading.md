**Passage(s):** 1 John 1:1–4, Greek per SBLGNT ([[tc-sblgnt]])
**Concept applied:** aspect · fronting · variant units

## Clause by clause (gloss mine, deliberately wooden)

**1** Ὃ ἦν ἀπ᾽ ἀρχῆς, — *That-which was from beginning,*
ὃ ἀκηκόαμεν, — *that-which we-have-heard,*
ὃ ἑωράκαμεν τοῖς ὀφθαλμοῖς ἡμῶν, — *…we-have-seen with the eyes of-us,*
ὃ ἐθεασάμεθα καὶ αἱ χεῖρες ἡμῶν ἐψηλάφησαν, — *…we-gazed-at, and the hands of-us handled,*
περὶ τοῦ λόγου τῆς ζωῆς— — *concerning the word of-the life—*
**2** καὶ ἡ ζωὴ ἐφανερώθη, καὶ ἑωράκαμεν καὶ μαρτυροῦμεν καὶ ἀπαγγέλλομεν ὑμῖν τὴν ζωὴν τὴν αἰώνιον ἥτις ἦν πρὸς τὸν πατέρα καὶ ἐφανερώθη ἡμῖν— — *and the life was-manifested, and we-have-seen and we-testify and we-announce to-you the life the eternal, which was toward the Father and was-manifested to-us—*
**3** ὃ ἑωράκαμεν καὶ ἀκηκόαμεν ἀπαγγέλλομεν καὶ ὑμῖν, ἵνα καὶ ὑμεῖς κοινωνίαν ἔχητε μεθ᾽ ἡμῶν· καὶ ἡ κοινωνία δὲ ἡ ἡμετέρα μετὰ τοῦ πατρὸς καὶ μετὰ τοῦ υἱοῦ αὐτοῦ Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ· — *that-which we-have-seen and we-have-heard we-announce also to-you, so-that also you may-have fellowship with us; and the fellowship, the our-own, (is) with the Father and with the Son of-him, Jesus Christ;*
**4** καὶ ταῦτα γράφομεν ἡμεῖς ἵνα ἡ χαρὰ ἡμῶν ᾖ πεπληρωμένη. — *and these-things we-write, we, so-that the joy of-us may-be having-been-filled.*

## What the Greek adds

1. **ὃ, not ὅς.** The relative is *neuter* ("that which") four times, not masculine ("he whom"): the object is a reality/message before it is a person — yet it is "handled." *Contested (emphasis):* Brown (Anchor Bible) takes the neuter as deliberately broad, the whole Christ-event; Marshall (NICNT) reads through it to the person.
2. **Three tense-forms, one sentence:** ἦν (imperfect) → ἀκηκόαμεν, ἑωράκαμεν (perfect) → ἐθεασάμεθα, ἐψηλάφησαν (aorist). *Majority view:* the choice encodes **aspect** — how the author frames the action — more than time. *Contested:* what "perfect" means (Porter: stative; Fanning: blended; Campbell: proximity) — [[decker-reading-koine-greek]].
3. **Main verb arrives late.** v.1's four ὃ-clauses are a fronted object with no verb; v.2 is a parenthesis (it does contain ἀπαγγέλλομεν); the sentence resolves only at v.3, ἀπαγγέλλομεν καὶ ὑμῖν. *Majority view (discourse grammar):* such fronting is marked — the backlog you carry is the point ([[runge-discourse-grammar]]).
4. **καί … καί … and κοινωνία.** v.2's repeated καί (polysyndeton) slows the line on purpose. κοινωνία is wider than "fellowship": association, *participation/sharing in*, even material contribution ([[bdag]]). Range *consensus*; dominant sense here *contested*.
5. **v.4: ἡμῶν or ὑμῶν** — "our joy" vs "your joy," one letter. UBS5/NA28 print **ἡμῶν** ([[tc-ubs5-greek-new-testament]]); **letter rating not verified** (a lay apparatus reports {B}). In later Koine η and υ converged in sound, so the two were homophones — a scribe copying by ear could not tell them apart.

**Traditions' readings:** Not a confessional flashpoint; divergence is historical, not doctrinal. The live question is the "we" — apostolic eyewitnesses, or a community voice claiming that tradition ([[authorship-and-dating-overview]]).

**Confidence:** (1) contested · (2) aspect-over-time majority view, perfect's sense contested · (3) majority view · (4) consensus range, contested sense · (5) textual: UBS5/NA28 ἡμῶν, rating not verified, both readings well attested.

## Do this (Stage 0 — alphabet is enough)

1. In [[step-bible]], open 1 John 1 (SBLGNT); hover-parse every verb in vv.1–4 and note only the tense label. Don't translate.
2. Find all six relatives (ὃ ×4 in v.1, ἥτις in v.2, ὃ in v.3); mark which are neuter and ask what noun each could agree with.
3. Find where v.1's sentence finally gets its main verb — count the words you had to hold.
