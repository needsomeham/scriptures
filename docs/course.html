Ready for Stage 1 when

Goal — Morphology + core syntax, reading-first.

Stage 1 has no fixed day plan — it runs on the weekly loop: 5× reading · 2× grammar · 1× lecture.
Scope caveat: Athenaze and similar reading-first primers teach Attic, not Koine — skip them for this path.
Ready for Stage 2 when

Goal — Sustained reading of real NT Greek, references introduced.

Stage 2 has no fixed day plan — it runs on the weekly loop: 5× reading · 2× grammar · 1× lecture.
Reading order: 1 John → Mark → John → shorter Pauline letters (Philippians, 1 Thessalonians).
Ready for Stage 3 when

Goal — Read with the apparatus, not just the running text; broaden into aspect/discourse theory and the LXX.

Stage 3 has no fixed day plan — it runs on the weekly loop: 5× reading · 2× grammar · 1× lecture.
No fixed end. Pick a variant from your current reading and look up why the committee decided as it did.

Decide once: pronunciation

SBL 2011 report · neutral overview · T2b
Erasmian is historically inaccurate but is what nearly every free video course uses; restored Koine is closer to first-century sound. Neither affects grammar. Default: Erasmian.
