In one line: A web study-Bible platform with Greek text display, parsing pop-ups, and "arcing/bracketing" diagram tools for visualizing sentence structure.

What it is: A commercial Bible-study platform (Biblearc, part of the same team behind Bethlehem College & Seminary-adjacent tools) offering the GNT with morphology overlays plus structural diagramming exercises and a self-paced "Greek I" course (equip.biblearc.com).

Use in the path: Stage 2–3 — the arcing/bracketing exercises are a genuinely reading-oriented way to internalize Greek clause structure and discourse flow (complements runge-discourse-grammar) rather than isolated parsing drills.

Strengths: Visual sentence-structure work reinforces syntax in context; free tier is usable for the biblical text itself.

Weaknesses / cautions: Full course and advanced diagramming features are paywalled; not independently peer-reviewed — treat pedagogical claims as one platform's method, not scholarly consensus.

Claims to note: none load-bearing beyond its own teaching method.
