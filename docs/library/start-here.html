Start here (Stage 0 kickoff for Jacob)

You know the English NT well and think in engineering terms. Good — Greek here rewards that: pattern recognition over memorization, reading over flashcards. This page is the concrete on-ramp; learning-path has the full curriculum. Everything below is free.

(a) Decide once: pronunciation

See pronunciation-erasmian-vs-restored-koine (https://danielstreett.com/2011/12/01/the-great-greek-pronunciation-debate-sbl-2011-report-pt-3/) for the full neutral overview. Short version:

  • Erasmian — 16th-c. reconstruction, historically inaccurate but it's

what nearly every seminary, textbook, and free video course uses.

  • Restored Koine — linguistically closer to how 1st-century speakers

actually sounded (majority view among historical phonologists), used by immersion programs like biblical-language-center-living-koine-greek.

  • Neither affects grammar or comprehension — it's purely how you voice

the letters.

  • Trade-off: restored Koine is the historically "truer" sound; Erasmian

is the practically compatible one.

  • Default: Erasmian, because daily-dose-of-greek and

biblicaltraining-mounce-nt201 (your Weeks 1–2 tools below) both use it. Switching later costs you a few weeks of re-hearing sounds, not months — it is cheap to change once you have reasons to.

(b) Set up (30 min)

  • step-bible — interlinear/morphology/lexicon platform.

https://www.stepbible.org/ — bookmark it now, you'll use it in Week 2.

  • logeion — free multi-dictionary Greek lexicon (Univ. of

Chicago). https://logeion.uchicago.edu/ — bookmark for later stages.

  • daily-dose-of-greek — daily verse videos (Rob Plummer, PhD).

Site: https://dailydoseofgreek.com/ — subscribe by entering your name/email on the homepage (5 weekly 2-min videos), or use the free iOS/Android app for daily notifications + full archive.

  • biblicaltraining-mounce-nt201 — free 36-lecture video course.

https://www.biblicaltraining.org/learn/institute/nt201-biblical-greek — create a free account to track progress.

  • billmounce-site — Mounce's own free lecture/vocab hub.

https://www.billmounce.com/

  • Bible Hub interlinear (T3, quick fallback only — verify anything

load-bearing against STEP or a lexicon; see step-bible): https://biblehub.com/interlinear/1_john/1-1.htm

  • Free Greek text online — pick one to skim now, you'll read it for

real in Stage 2: tc-sblgnt https://sblgnt.com/ (download page; documented eclectic text) or tc-tyndale-house-greek-new-testament https://thegreeknewtestament.com/ (fresh independent edition, sample of Mark free online).

(c) Weeks 1–2, day by day (10–25 min/day)

  1. Day 1 (20 min): Alphabet chunk 1 — α β γ δ ε ζ η θ (names + sounds,

Erasmian). Use mounce-basics-of-biblical-greek ch. 1 or greek-alphabet-and-diacritics as your reference table.

  1. Day 2 (20 min): Alphabet chunk 2 — ι κ λ μ ν ξ ο π. Say each aloud.
  2. Day 3 (20 min): Alphabet chunk 3 — ρ σ/ς τ υ φ χ ψ ω. Full alphabet

review, uppercase + lowercase + final sigma.

  1. Day 4 (15 min): Breathings (smooth/rough) and the iota subscript —

see greek-alphabet-and-diacritics. Practice on 5 real NT words.

  1. Day 5 (15 min): The three accents (acute/grave/circumflex) — what

they mark, why you can mostly ignore them for now (pitch, not stress, in the historical system). Read greek-alphabet-and-diacritics.

  1. Day 6 (15 min): Read the whole alphabet aloud, uppercase and

lowercase, from memory. Time yourself; repeat until under 60 seconds.

  1. Day 7 (10 min): First daily-dose-of-greek video — pick any

verse from the archive, just get the habit started. (https://dailydoseofgreek.com/)

  1. Day 8 (25 min): biblicaltraining-mounce-nt201 Lecture 1 — "The

Greek Language" (alphabet, pronunciation, grammar overview).

  1. Day 9 (25 min): NT201 Lecture 3 — "The Alphabet and Pronunciation"

(gamma nasals, vowels, diphthongs, breathing marks in more depth).

  1. Day 10 (20 min): Transcribe 1 John 1:1 letter-by-letter by hand

from a printed/screen Greek text (e.g. via step-bible or tc-sblgnt) — slow, deliberate, no typing.

  1. Day 11 (20 min): Second daily-dose-of-greek video; note any

letters/sounds still shaky and drill those specifically.

  1. Day 12 (25 min): Open step-bible

(https://www.stepbible.org/) and hover-parse every word of 1 John 1:1–4 — just look, don't try to memorize the grammar terms yet.

  1. Day 13 (15 min): NT201 Lecture 4 — "Punctuation and

Syllabification." Re-read your Day 10 transcription and check it against the syllable rules.

  1. Day 14 (15 min): Read 1 John 1:1–4 aloud, Erasmian pronunciation,

from the text you transcribed. Don't worry about comprehension — this is decoding fluency. This is the same passage 1-john-1-1-4-first-reading walks through in more depth.

(d) Then

Move to Stage 1 in learning-path once you can write all 24 letters + diacritics from memory and have STEP Bible + Logeion bookmarked. For a guided first real reading, see 1-john-1-1-4-first-reading.
