Metzger, "A Textual Commentary on the Greek New Testament" (UBS)

Source
SourceT1intermediatepaid (~$40-60)Textual criticism#reference, #apparatus-companion

In one line: The essential companion to NA28/UBS5 — explains, verse by verse, why the UBS committee chose each rated reading over its rivals.

What it is: Written by Bruce M. Metzger as the official rationale volume for the UBS committee's decisions (1971, 2nd ed. 1994); keyed directly to UBS's lettered variants (A-D confidence ratings). For every variant UBS rates, Metzger records the committee's reasoning and, often, dissenting committee members' views [T-11].

Use in the path: Read the relevant entry every time you hit a UBS-flagged variant in real reading — this is the single highest-leverage habit for this learner's stated goal ("read the NA28 apparatus with Metzger's Commentary beside it"), ideally starting once apparatus symbols are familiar (~month 6+).

Strengths: Direct access to editorial reasoning (internal vs. external evidence, transcriptional probability) rather than just the verdict; models exactly the reasoned-eclectic method in tc-reasoned-eclecticism; covers all tc-major-disputed-passages in this vault.

Weaknesses / cautions: Reflects the UBS4-era committee (pre-UBS5 minor rating revisions in places); occasionally states committee disagreement as a bare vote count without full argument — cross-check contested calls against tc-hixson-gurry-myths-and-mistakes or tc-parker-introduction-nt-manuscripts for pushback.

Claims to note: Model instance of the mandatory textual-confidence line — every entry states a UBS letter rating; use these ratings directly when writing tc-major-disputed-passages bridges.
