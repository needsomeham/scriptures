Gap ledger

Anything a researcher couldn't verify, couldn't find, or found contested goes here with a priority: P1 blocks learning, P2 matters, P3 nice-to-have. Append rows to the matching section; don't rewrite this file wholesale. Row format:

- [P?] <gap> — <why> — <suggested next step>

P1 — blocks learning

  • [P1] García Martínez DSS translation not yet its own note — bg-vermes-dead-sea-scrolls-english covers the standard translation, but if DSS becomes central to the path a second scholarly-translation note may be warranted — add if/when DSS reading moves from Stage 3 sampling to core.
  • [P1] NA29 has no announced publication date — the field's next standard critical text is in progress at Münster — watch die-bibel.de and update tc-na28-novum-testamentum-graece when announced.

P2 — matters

  • [P2] Biblingo curriculum-author credentials unverified — biblingo is filed T3 pending verification — check for named linguists/scholars on staff before recommending above free alternatives.
  • [P2] 2026 pricing for biblingo, ancient-language-institute-koine, biblical-language-center-living-koine-greek is approximate — re-check current published prices before Jacob commits money.
  • [P2] Cross-check the Mounce bio line in mounce-basics-of-biblical-greek / billmounce-site against a primary faculty/CV page.
  • [P2] bdag and louw-nida-lexicon prices are retailer estimates, not publisher list prices — verify before budgeting.
  • [P2] Barrett, New Testament Background: Selected Documents not catalogued (available on Internet Archive) — a useful low-cost primary-source anthology alongside bg-keener-ivp-bible-background-commentary.
  • [P2] Bible Odyssey (SBL) and CCEL's Early Church Fathers collection verified as free but have no source notes yet — both are credible T2b candidates for a future wave.
  • [P2] Malina & Rohrbaugh's Gospel of John volume not catalogued — only the Synoptics volume (bg-malina-rohrbaugh-social-science-commentary) is; add if the path reaches John in earnest.
  • [P2] Elwell & Yarbrough, Readings from the First-Century World — unverified as a currently-available/accurate title; check before citing.
  • [P2] No material-culture/archaeology source yet in the background domain — a gap for Stage 2–3 (e.g. an archaeology-of-the-NT-world text or site).
  • [P2] Wasserman & Gurry's CBGM introduction (tc-wasserman-gurry-cbgm) is not open access, and no free equivalent of the same technical depth exists — tc-cbgm (concept note) and the INTF's own overview are the free substitutes but shallower.
  • [P2] Byzantine-priority open-access scholarship is thin — Robinson's JBTC article (cited in tc-byzantine-priority-debate) is the best free primary statement; most other Byzantine-priority work is print-only.
  • [P2] Greenlee and/or Black beginner textual-criticism introductions (common seminary-intro alternatives to Metzger & Ehrman) not catalogued — worth a look for a gentler on-ramp than tc-metzger-ehrman-text-of-the-new-testament.
  • [P2] Muratorian Fragment date is contested among canon historians — tc-canon-formation-overview should cite McDonald/Metzger, The Canon of the New Testament, for the range of scholarly dating rather than asserting one date.
  • [P2] Cross-link step-bible to the textual-criticism domain if it turns out to host THGNT text/apparatus directly — verify and add the link from the TC side, not just the Greek-learning side.
  • [P2] UBS5 letter rating for 1 John 1:4 ἡμῶν (vs ὑμῶν) not verified against Metzger's Textual Commentary on the GNT (T1) — 1-john-1-1-4-first-reading currently reports a lay apparatus's {B} secondhand — check Metzger directly and fill the confirmed rating into the bridge note.
  • [P2] Brown, The Epistles of John (Anchor Bible) and Marshall, The Epistles of John (NICNT) are cited in 1-john-1-1-4-first-reading but have no sources/ note/ref-ID rows yet — catalogue both as T1/T2 source notes.

P3 — nice-to-have

  • [P3] Danker's Concise Greek-English Lexicon is folded into the bdag note rather than split out — split into its own note if Jacob wants a lighter/cheaper lexicon distinct from full BDAG.
  • [P3] No verified free Coursera/edX Koine Greek course taught by a named scholar was found — most free MOOCs in this space have lapsed; keep watching.
  • [P3] GNTreader.com and Kata Biblon were surfaced in search but not independently verified for credentials/tier — do not cite until checked.
  • [P3] SBLGNT's license (sblgnt.com) should be re-verified directly before any future dataset use beyond reading — open-greek-nt-datasets currently reports terms secondhand.
  • [P3] Early Christian Writings (earlychristianwritings.com) not catalogued as its own source note, though it's linked as a translation host from bg-philo-yonge-works.
  • [P3] Oxford Biblical Studies Online is likely institutional-subscription-only — confirm before ever recommending it as if it were open.
  • [P3] A second free university course on Second Temple Judaism / Dead Sea Scrolls (beyond Vermes's translation) has not been identified.
  • [P3] Patronage as a topic could be split out of roman-imperial-context into its own concept note if the path needs finer granularity than the current combined treatment.
  • [P3] No open Greek→English parallel-text dataset has been surveyed yet for the far-off LLM goal — open-greek-nt-datasets covers Greek-only tagged text, not parallel translation data.
  • [P3] No OCW (open courseware) lecture series specifically on NT textual transmission has been identified — bg-yale-dale-martin-lectures touches it in passing but isn't TC-focused.
  • [P3] Aland & Aland's The Text of the New Testament (cited as [T-17]) details are from prior knowledge and should be re-verified against a current edition/printing before load-bearing citation.
  • [P3] Consider a bridges/ note per Stage 2 reading milestone (e.g. 1 John 2, Mark 1) once Jacob is reading that far — mirrors 1-john-1-1-4-first-reading's format.

Next wave candidates

Focused follow-up waves, roughly in priority order:

  1. Free reference sites — verify and catalogue Bible Odyssey (SBL), CCEL Early Church Fathers, Early Christian Writings, and confirm/reject GNTreader.com and Kata Biblon as citable T2b sources. Closes several P2/P3 gaps above at once.
  2. Beginner textual-criticism intros — survey Greenlee, Black, and any current open-access "TC 101" material to give Stage 2 readers a gentler on-ramp before tc-metzger-ehrman-text-of-the-new-testament.
  3. LLM-relevant open datasets — a dedicated pass surveying parallel Greek-English aligned corpora and licensing, distinct from the tagged-Greek-only datasets already in open-greek-nt-datasets. Out of scope for Phase 1 learning but worth a scouting wave given the stated far-off goal.
