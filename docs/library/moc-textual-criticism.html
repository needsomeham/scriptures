MOC — Textual criticism

Every sources/tc-*.md and concepts/tc-*.md note, grouped by sub-topic. See [[learning-path]] Stage 2–3 for placement, and _control/references-textual.md for the bibliography shard (grep by [T-NN]; don't read end-to-end).

Manuscripts & databases

  • tc-text-types-and-manuscript-families — Alexandrian/Byzantine/

Western/Caesarean clustering, concept overview.

  • tc-csntm — free high-res manuscript photo archive.
  • tc-codex-sinaiticus-project — full free digitization of Sinaiticus.
  • tc-ntvmr-intf-ecm — Münster's manuscript database + in-progress ECM.

Critical editions

  • tc-na28-novum-testamentum-graece — standard scholarly text/apparatus.
  • tc-ubs5-greek-new-testament — same text, translator-oriented

apparatus with {A}–{D} confidence ratings.

  • tc-sblgnt — free, born-digital eclectic text.
  • tc-tyndale-house-greek-new-testament — independent 2017 critical

edition, free online.

  • tc-robinson-pierpont-byzantine-textform — standard Byzantine/

Majority text edition.

  • tc-westcott-hort-nestle1904 — historical public-domain ancestors of

NA/UBS.

Apparatus reading & major variants

  • tc-reading-a-critical-apparatus — how to read NA28/UBS5 sigla.
  • tc-metzger-textual-commentary-gnt — editorial rationale for every

UBS-rated variant; the essential apparatus companion.

  • tc-major-disputed-passages — Mark 16:9-20, John 7:53–8:11, 1 John

5:7, and other notable variants.

Method

  • tc-reasoned-eclecticism — dominant modern method (+ thoroughgoing

eclecticism).

  • tc-byzantine-priority-debate — the live methodological fault line.
  • tc-cbgm — Coherence-Based Genealogical Method, concept overview.
  • tc-wasserman-gurry-cbgm — most accessible technical CBGM intro

(paid, not open access — see gaps).

Surveys & popular treatments

  • tc-metzger-ehrman-text-of-the-new-testament — standard graduate

survey of TC history/method.

  • tc-parker-introduction-nt-manuscripts — advanced, manuscript-

centered survey.

  • tc-hixson-gurry-myths-and-mistakes — corrects overclaiming on both

apologetic and skeptical sides.

  • tc-ehrman-misquoting-jesus — widely-read popular account; facts

generally accurate, framing contested.

  • tc-evangelical-textual-criticism-blog — scholar-run current-

awareness blog (T3, orient only).

Canon formation

  • tc-canon-formation-overview — criteria, timeline, disputed books.
