MOC — NT historical background

Every sources/bg-*.md and background concepts/*.md note, grouped by sub-topic. See [[learning-path]] Stage 2–3 for placement, and _control/references-background.md for the bibliography shard (grep by [B-NN]; don't read end-to-end).

Intro textbooks (spine options)

  • bg-ehrman-new-testament-historical-introduction — standard

secular-critical intro.

  • bg-powell-introducing-the-new-testament — mainline-Protestant,

narrative-critical emphasis.

  • bg-brown-introduction-to-new-testament — Catholic historical-critical

benchmark.

  • bg-wright-bird-new-testament-in-its-world — evangelical-critical,

"worldview" framing.

  • bg-johnson-writings-of-the-new-testament — Catholic, literary/

theological counterweight.

Video/audio courses

  • bg-yale-dale-martin-lectures — free full Open Yale course (Stage 1

parallel track).

  • bg-nt-pod-goodacre — short podcast episodes, named scholar.
  • bg-bibleproject-nt-overviews — free orientation-only videos (T3,

not a citable claim source).
