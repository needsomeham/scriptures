Living Koine Greek (Randall Buth / Biblical Language Center)

Source
SourceT2bbeginnerpaid (~$150–300+ per part, audio course; live intensives extra)Greek learning#immersion, #audio, #course, #pronunciation

In one line: A comprehension-based, spoken-immersion Koine course by linguist Randall Buth, teaching Greek the way modern languages are taught rather than through translation drills.

What it is: Randall Buth (PhD linguistics, University of Florida) founded the Biblical Language Center to apply TPR/comprehensible-input methods to Koine Greek, using a restored/historical Koine pronunciation (see pronunciation-erasmian-vs-restored-koine). Materials are mostly audio+workbook, with occasional live intensive workshops.

Use in the path: Optional Stage 1–2 track for a learner who wants active/oral fluency alongside reading, not a replacement for grammar study. Best paired with, not instead of, a reading spine — it builds intuition for word order and aspect that grammar-translation methods (Mounce, Croy) approach more analytically.

Strengths: Distinctive comprehensible-input pedagogy, linguistically informed pronunciation choice, produces real fluency in production, not just parsing. <br>

Weaknesses / cautions: Expensive relative to free alternatives; restored-pronunciation choice is itself a contested pedagogical/historical question (see pronunciation note); smaller user base than mainstream seminary tracks means fewer study partners.

Claims to note: Restored historical Koine pronunciation is presented by BLC as more historically accurate than Erasmian — a majority view among historical phonologists for the period, but Erasmian remains the consensus classroom convention in most seminaries; see pronunciation-erasmian-vs-restored-koine.
